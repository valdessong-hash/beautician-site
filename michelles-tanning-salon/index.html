Good to know

Before your first visit

  • i

    Bookings in salon

    We take bookings in person rather than online, so you can see the rooms and we can set your time properly. Drop in during opening hours.

  • ii

    18+ and photo ID

    UK law prohibits anyone under 18 from using a commercial sunbed. We check ID from every new customer, without exception.

  • iii

    Twelve minutes, maximum

    No session runs longer than twelve minutes. If you are new to tanning we will start you well below that and build up.

  • iv

    Sunbed Association member

    We are a member salon, which means our equipment and operating standards are held to the association's code.

  • v

    Eyewear every session

    Closed eyelids do not block UV. Protective goggles are provided and are not optional.
