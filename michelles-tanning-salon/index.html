Good questions

Before you come in

How long should my first session be?
Usually three to six minutes, depending on your skin type. It will look like nothing happened. That is the point — UV keeps developing for up to 24 hours, and a cautious first session is what stops session three going wrong.
Do I need to book?
Walk in whenever you like and we will fit you in if a room is free. Weekday evenings and Saturday mornings get busy, so a quick call or message ahead saves you a wait.
Can I use a sunbed under 18?
No. The Sunbeds (Regulation) Act 2010 makes it illegal for a business in England and Wales to let anyone under 18 use, or even wait in the room with, a commercial sunbed. We ask for photo ID and we do not make exceptions.
How often can I tan?
Leave a minimum of 24 hours between UV sessions, and no more than two sessions in any 48 hours. Most people hold a good colour on two or three sessions a week once they have built up.
Do I have to wear goggles?
Yes. Closed eyelids do not block UV, and there is no melanin in your cornea to protect it. We supply approved eyewear free on your first visit and sell your own pair for a couple of pounds after that.
Sunbed or spray tan before a holiday?
Spray, if you only want colour in the photos. A base tan is not meaningful sun protection — it is worth roughly SPF 3 — so pack the factor 30 either way.
