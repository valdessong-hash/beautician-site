Sunbeds

Nine ways to catch the light

Different beds do genuinely different jobs. Here is what each one is for, roughly how long you should be on it, and who it does not suit.

Stand-up

Turbo Stand-up

A vertical booth with high-output lamps and cold air blowing the whole time. Because nothing presses against your skin you get no pale patches down your back or the backs of your thighs — the single most common complaint about lie-down beds.

6–12 minVery evenCoolQuickest results

Best for: anyone in a hurry, anyone who finds lie-down beds claustrophobic.

Lie-down

Luxury Lie-down

Body cooling, facial tanners you can turn down or off entirely, and Bluetooth audio. Slower and gentler than the stand-up, and the one people book when they want the twenty minutes to themselves as much as the colour.

9–18 minAdjustable facialsAirconAudio

Best for: building a base slowly, or tanning legs that never seem to catch up.

Gentle

Low-pressure Base Bed

More UVB in the mix, which is what actually kicks off melanin production. Slower to show colour than a high-pressure bed but it builds a base that holds, rather than one that washes off in a week.

8–15 minBuilds baseLonger lasting

Best for: first-timers and fair skin working up carefully.

UV free

Collagen & Red Light

Red and near-infrared light with no ultraviolet whatsoever, so there is no tanning and no burn risk. Used for skin tone, texture and post-exercise recovery. Safe to use daily, and safe on the same day as a UV session.

15–20 minNo UVDaily use ok

Best for: anyone who wants the lounge without the ultraviolet.

Top-up

High-pressure Finisher

Heavy on UVA, which darkens the melanin you already have. It is a top-up bed, not a starter bed — there is little point booking it until you have a base to deepen.

10–15 minDeepens colourBase required

Best for: the week before you fly.

No UV

Spray Tan Booth

A private room with extraction, barrier cream, disposables and somewhere to hang your clothes. Hand applied by us rather than an automated nozzle, which is why the colour follows your shape.

15 minHand appliedPrivate

Getting more from it

Small things that double the payoff

  • Exfoliate the day before, not the day of. Dead skin holds colour badly and sheds it fast.
  • Come in without make-up, fake tan or perfume. Anything on your skin either blocks UV or reacts under it.
  • Use a tan accelerator. It is not a gimmick — hydrated skin tans measurably better and holds it for longer.
  • Moisturise the same evening, then every day after. Dry skin flakes, and flaking is what makes a tan look patchy.
  • Leave 24 hours between sessions. Your colour is still developing while you sleep.
  • Wear the goggles. Every time, all the way through.
