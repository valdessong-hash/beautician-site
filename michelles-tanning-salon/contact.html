Before you visit

Quick answers

What do I need to bring on a first visit?
Photo ID and about ten minutes. We will run through a short skin assessment, set a sensible starting time and give you eyewear.
Is there a card machine?
Yes — card, contactless, Apple Pay and cash. Minute bundles can be topped up at the desk any time.
Can I bring a friend?
Of course, and there is seating at the front. Anyone under 18 cannot come into the room with a sunbed, though — that part is law.
Do you do gift vouchers?
Yes, from £10 upwards, for minutes or for a specific treatment. They do not expire.
How late can I turn up?
The last session starts twenty minutes before we close, so we are not rushing you out mid-tan.
