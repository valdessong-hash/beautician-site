Before your appointment

Prep is most of the result

A great solution on badly prepped skin still looks bad. Twenty minutes of preparation the night before is worth more than any upgrade you can buy.

  • Shave or wax 24 hours before, not on the day. Open follicles trap solution and leave dots.
  • Exfoliate the night before with a scrub that has no oil in it. Oil leaves a film that blocks colour.
  • Skip moisturiser on the day except on knees, elbows, ankles and knuckles, where a thin layer stops those spots grabbing.
  • Come bare-faced and without deodorant, perfume or SPF.
  • Bring dark, loose clothing and flip-flops. Tight waistbands and bra straps will press lines into a developing tan.

Afterwards

Making it last ten days

Spray tan sits in the very top layer of your skin. Everything that speeds up shedding also speeds up fading.

  • Wait for your develop time before the first rinse — warm water only, no soap, no scrubbing.
  • Pat dry. Rubbing with a towel lifts colour straight off.
  • Moisturise twice a day from the second day. This is the single biggest factor in how it fades.
  • Avoid long baths, chlorine and steam rooms for the first 48 hours.
  • Skip the exfoliation until you actually want it gone, then scrub and it lifts evenly.
A spray tan gives you no sun protection at all. The colour is a reaction in dead surface cells — it does not involve melanin and it will not stop you burning. Wear SPF exactly as you would with pale skin.

Spray tan questions

The ones we get daily

How long does it take?
About fifteen minutes in the booth, plus a couple of minutes talking through depth and aftercare. Allow half an hour for a first appointment.
What do I wear?
Whatever you are comfortable in. Disposables are provided, plenty of people go without, and it is a private room either way.
Will it turn orange?
Orange comes from a depth that is wrong for your skin, or from prep that was skipped. We solve the first by matching properly and the second by telling you what to do the night before.
How long before an event should I book?
Two days ahead is the sweet spot. Day one it is still settling, day two it looks its best, and you have a buffer if anything needs adjusting.
Is it vegan?
Yes. Every solution we use is vegan, cruelty-free and paraben-free, with a DHA base derived from sugar cane.
Can I have one while pregnant?
Many people do, and DHA is not absorbed beyond the surface layer of skin. We still ask you to check with your midwife first, and we will not spray in the first trimester.

Event on Saturday?

Book for Thursday. We keep late slots back for exactly this.
