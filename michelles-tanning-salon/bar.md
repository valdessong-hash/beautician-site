# bar.md — the craft bar

## Provenance, stated honestly

Both named reference URLs (beautique.axiomthemes.com, service.totaltanning.co.uk/uv)
are blocked by this environment's egress policy — 403 at the proxy CONNECT. I have
never rendered either one. So this bar is NOT a teardown of those two sites.

It is derived from the one reference I can actually see: the screenshot of
michellestanninglounge.co.uk, plus its brand assets (the wordmark, the printed
price-list card). Where the two URLs would have set the bar, craft fundamentals do.
The craft critic is therefore judging against Michelle's own identity and these
mechanisms — not against the two sites. That limitation is real and is reported.

## Mechanisms — each checkable by looking

1. **True black, not charcoal.** Ground is #000000, matching the existing site and
   the price-list card. Panels lift by no more than #111. No grey-blue tints.

2. **Gold is scarce.** Metallic gold (#C9A24B family) appears at most three times
   per viewport: one hairline, one label, one CTA. Body text is cream, never gold.
   Gold on gold is a fail.

3. **Three type sizes visible per section, maximum.** Display serif, body, and a
   letterspaced small-caps label (0.2em+). The wordmark script is a fourth face used
   only in the logo lockup, nowhere else.

4. **Headline is at least 4x body size** at desktop. The existing site's hero is
   undersized for its own confidence — "Luxury Tanning Beds in East Swindon"
   deserves to dominate.

5. **Rules, not boxes.** Separation comes from 1px gold hairlines and whitespace.
   A border-radius card with a shadow is reserved for exactly one element: the
   price list, which echoes the real printed card. Everything else is flat.

6. **Nothing animates under 500ms, nothing bounces.** Premium reads as slow and
   resolved. Fades and 20px rises only — no scale-ins, no springs, no marquee
   faster than 40s.

7. **Vertical rhythm is generous.** Section padding at least 6rem mobile / 10rem
   desktop. The existing site's sections are stacked too tightly to read as luxury.

## Content rules (from the real site — non-negotiable)

- Bookings are **in salon only**. No booking form, no fake availability calendar.
- 18+ only, photo ID required.
- Member of The Sunbed Association.
- Greenbridge, East Swindon — "the area's only dedicated tanning bed salon".
- Real address, hours, phone, email and prices only. No invented figures.
